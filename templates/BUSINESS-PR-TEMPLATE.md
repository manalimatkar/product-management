# Business PR Template

Reusable format implementing [BUSINESS-PR-SPEC.md](../specs/BUSINESS-PR-SPEC.md). Use this as the PR description (or equivalent review-package document) for a Business PR.

> This PR is generated by the configured Business Agent from an approved Design Analysis and approved Business Requirements. It defines business intent and observable behavior only. It does not define technical implementation. It is a draft until the named Business Owner approves it.

## 11. Pre-Review Quality Gate

*(Business Agent self-check before requesting review -- does not substitute for Business Owner approval.)*

- [ ] Source, Design Analysis, and Requirements versions are recorded and consistent
- [ ] Every Story has a complete Stage Trace row (section 4)
- [ ] No Stage Trace, requirement, or Story cell names a framework, component, API, endpoint, or schema
- [ ] Every unresolved Assumption and Decision from upstream artifacts is still listed (section 7-8), not silently resolved
- [ ] Acceptance criteria are observable in business language
- [ ] Scope and exclusions are explicit (section 9)
- [ ] This PR is clearly marked as generated content pending Business Owner approval
