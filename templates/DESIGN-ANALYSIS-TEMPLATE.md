## 17. Quality Checklist

- [ ] Exact source paths and versions are recorded.
- [ ] Analysis version is recorded.
- [ ] All reviewed source material is listed.
- [ ] Actors, goals, domain concepts, capabilities, workflows, and outcomes are recorded.
- [ ] Screens, pages, navigation, actions, inputs, outputs, and states are inventoried.
- [ ] User journeys include alternate and error paths where represented.
- [ ] Every significant conclusion has evidence.
- [ ] Classifications and confidence levels are present.
- [ ] Strong inferences are labeled `Strongly Implied`.
- [ ] Assumptions are separated from requirements.
- [ ] Business decisions are explicitly listed rather than invented.
- [ ] Technical unknowns are handed to the Technical Agent.
- [ ] Requirements are atomic, observable, and testable.
- [ ] Acceptance criteria describe business behavior, not implementation.
- [ ] Conflicts and missing information are visible.
- [ ] Requirements map to capabilities, Stories, and design evidence.
- [ ] Technical implementation choices are excluded.
