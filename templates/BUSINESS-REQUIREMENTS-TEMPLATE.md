## 10. Review Checklist

- [ ] Exact source material and version are recorded.
- [ ] Analysis artifact, type, and version are recorded when applicable.
- [ ] Business problem and desired outcome are clear.
- [ ] Actors, scope, exclusions, and dependencies are identified.
- [ ] Requirements describe observable business behavior.
- [ ] Every generated requirement has evidence and classification.
- [ ] Business rules, assumptions, and decisions are visible.
- [ ] Acceptance criteria are observable and cover applicable states.
- [ ] Technical implementation choices are excluded.
- [ ] Traceability from evidence to requirements is complete.
- [ ] Related parent and downstream item references are recorded when available.
- [ ] Required configured approval is recorded or marked `Pending`.
- [ ] Version record, change level, and predecessor are recorded.
- [ ] Affected requirements and downstream items are identified.
